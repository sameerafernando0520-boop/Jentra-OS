# Wave 5 forecasts and exact ratios

Implementation scope authorized by the owner's October 6 instruction to finish Wave 5. This additive synthetic contract implements FR-509–515. It does not authorize installation, governed export changes or live financial provider activation.

Forecast version 1 freezes a complete scoped current opportunity observation: original opportunity ID/version, amount at six decimals, probability as an exact decimal percentage from zero through 100, currency and explicit expected-close date. Its weighted amount is `amount × probability / 100`, rounded half away from zero at six decimals per opportunity before summing. Amounts cannot be negative. Results group by currency; no implicit FX conversion or mixed-currency total is allowed. The horizon is half open and at most 366 UTC days, with at most 10,000 sources and 100 currencies. A real zero remains zero; empty populations are explicit.

The current CRM source is mutable and lacks certified historical amount, stage-entry and ownership histories. It supports a frozen current observation only. Historical conversion, stage aging, velocity and forecast accuracy remain unavailable until their specific historical/realized mapping providers are reviewed. Reading a current status cannot invent that evidence. A complete snapshot must retain a canonical digest and exact source versions; the trusted server adapter, not a client flag, establishes permission/completeness. Reopen rechecks current permissions.

Ratio version 1 consumes one authoritative statement publication whose scope, period, currency, formula revision and source identity are explicit. Exact integer numerator and denominator are returned alongside a six-decimal quotient using half-away rounding. Formula-specific sign constraints must be supplied; zero or disallowed negative denominators are unavailable, never infinity or a fabricated zero. Unpublished/incomplete, differently scoped, differently dated or differently denominated statement inputs fail the affected basis. A domain value is not proof that an authoritative statement provider is installed.

Cash scenarios similarly need a reviewed dated-control and remaining-obligation provider. Current bank/control, AR, AP and payroll availability must be reported independently. No available balance or projection may be synthesized when an obligation family is missing. Scenario assumptions are immutable revisions and projected amounts have no journal/document mutation path. Export remains gated on the existing governed export provider's exact basis and current permission checks.

Acceptance requires deterministic exact arithmetic, rounding/tie and permutation cases, source/version/mixed-currency/zero/empty/limit failures, gateway permission/provider checks, native/recovery/rendering and installed/authenticated/load evidence. These gates remain separate.
